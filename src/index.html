Search tips: type several words to narrow down, e.g. borg maadi, pharmacy zayed, scan nasr. Spelling variants are tolerated (Mohandessin / Mohandeseen, Shoubra / Shubra, Tagamoa → New Cairo, Masr El Gedida → Heliopolis).

Click a district or brand tag on any card to filter by it. Your filters are kept in the address bar, so Share view copies a link to exactly what you see.

On each card: Call dials the first number, Map opens Google Maps, and ⋯ More lets you share (WhatsApp etc.), copy details, add a private note, or add your own tags like #my lab.

Recent (in the Show filter) lists the last 20 facilities you called, mapped, copied or shared. Switch between Cards and the compact List in the toolbar.

PDF: builds a designed PDF (cover, clickable contents, table or card layout, tappable phone numbers and map links) from your current view, your favourites or everything — preview it, then download. Works offline.
